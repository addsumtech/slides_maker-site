# Michael Jackson — The King of Pop (Career Retrospective)

A 14-slide figure-profile / tribute deck built as a public gallery example for slide-maker.
Dark "stage / spotlight" editorial system, 16:9, English. Every visual is native — no
AI-generated imagery and no real-person portraits — so the template renders identically
anywhere and carries none of the fidelity risk of generated faces.

## Template File

`template.pptx` (10 × 5.625 in, 16:9) — fully editable native PowerPoint: real text runs,
vector shapes, and recolored SVG icons. Regenerate any time from `build_mj.py` (the source of
truth). Slide PNGs are in `render/` (`slide01.png` … `slide14.png`).

## Summary

A career retrospective on Michael Jackson — from the Jackson 5 to solo stardom, the landmark
albums, the stage- and music-video innovations, the cultural impact, and, handled soberly and
neutrally, the controversy and contested legacy.

This is a **real-content example, not a placeholder deck**: every date, figure, and claim was
web-verified (Billboard, Wikipedia, GRAMMY.com, RIAA, Rock & Roll Hall of Fame, Britannica,
Variety), and where numbers legitimately vary they are stated as estimates rather than invented.
The point of shipping it with true content is that the **visual system is the reusable asset** —
the palette, the Didot + Helvetica pairing, the chrome, and the native motifs (spotlight beam,
vinyl record, film strip, staircase, lighting truss) transfer directly to any figure profile or
tribute talk. **Swap the content, keep the design:** replace Michael Jackson's milestones with
another subject's and the deck still holds together.

It fits any "profile of a person / body of work" talk: an artist or founder retrospective, an
in-memoriam or tribute, an award or honoree introduction, a "story of X" keynote — anywhere a
single figure's arc, landmark works, impact, and legacy need to read in seconds on a dark,
cinematic stage.

## Structure

1. **Cover** — "Michael Jackson · The King of Pop, 1958–2009"; spotlight beam, example-deck marker.
2. **Orientation** — the thesis ("one life, measured in firsts") beside three anchor stats: 400M+ records, 13 solo #1s, 67M Thriller.
3. **The Jackson 5** — Motown 1968; the four consecutive #1 singles shown as an ascending staircase.
4. **Off the Wall (1979)** — the solo pivot with Quincy Jones; native vinyl-record motif + spec table.
5. **Thriller (1982)** — the record-breaker (money slide): hero "67M" + tiles for 8 Grammys, 7 top-10 singles, 30× platinum.
6. **The moonwalk** — Motown 25 (aired May 16 1983); a quiet spotlight moment with one line of copy.
7. **The short film** — Thriller reinvented the music video; native film-strip motif + facts (John Landis, 14 min, National Film Registry).
8. **The solo catalog** — six studio albums 1979–2001 on a horizontal timeline, Bad highlighted (first album with five #1s).
9. **The stage** — Super Bowl XXVII (1993): 133.4M viewers, the first halftime to raise the ratings; lighting-truss motif.
10. **Beyond the charts** — three impact pillars (MTV barrier · "We Are the World" · the global blueprint) with recolored icons.
11. **Recognition** — an honor roll: 8 Grammys (1984), Grammy Legend (1993), Rock Hall (1997 & 2001), 13/17 #1s.
12. **The contested record** — controversy, strictly neutral and factual (1994 civil settlement · 2005 acquittal · 2019 Leaving Neverland); the accent gold is deliberately withdrawn to ash here.
13. **Legacy** — "the template for the modern pop star"; a large serif statement with the vinyl motif returning as a callback.
14. **Closing** — bookend of the cover ("Long live the King of Pop"), the reusable-system line, and the sources list.

## Fonts & Colors

**Fonts**
- **Didot** — display / titles / the serif statement lines. Chosen for a glamorous, timeless
  "retrospective" register. (macOS system font; substitutes to another high-contrast serif —
  e.g. Bodoni 72, Didot on Windows via a swap — if absent. Swap to Georgia/"Playfair Display"
  for full portability.)
- **Helvetica Neue** — all body copy, labels, chrome, and every numeral. A lining-figure face,
  so the big stats (67M, 133.4M, 400M+) sit clean and baseline-aligned. Falls back to Arial.
- Editable native text throughout (no rasterized headings), so the whole deck is re-typable.

**Colors** — one dark canvas, one semantic accent.
- Background `#0E0D13` (warm near-black — stage darkness); panels `#1C1826`; faint rules `#352E44`.
- **Gold `#E8B23A` is the single semantic accent = Michael's artistry / the spotlight.** It is
  bound to that one meaning deck-wide (eyebrows, hero numerals, key words, the beam), never used
  as generic decoration.
- Text: ivory `#F3EFE6` (primary) · slate `#CBC5D2` (secondary) · muted `#958EA0` (captions / chrome).
- **The one deliberate deviation:** on the controversy slide (12) the gold is **withdrawn to ash /
  graphite** (`#8E8A96`). Celebration has a color; the contested chapter does not — the palette
  itself signals the change in register.
